This tracker lists every gas flare mapped in Iraq alongside two independent datasets: its satellite activity status (when NASA VIIRS last detected it burning, over the past 12 months) and the World Bank flaring volume at the facility it sits at (GGFR 2025).

The two sources measure different things. Flare locations come from OpenStreetMap and mark individual flare stacks. World Bank volumes are estimated per facility / field, not per stack. To show a figure against each flare, flares and World Bank facilities are grouped into field clusters by location (within 3 km), and each cluster's total volume is split evenly among the flares in it.
The per-flare estimated share is an indicative allocation, not a measured per-flare figure — it assumes every flare in a cluster burns equally, which won't be exactly true. It is designed to sum correctly: adding up the share column reproduces the national total. Each flare also shows its cluster's real World Bank total, so the source figure is never hidden.
