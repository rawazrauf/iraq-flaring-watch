About Iraq Flaring Watch

When crude oil is extracted, natural gas comes up with it. Operators burn it off — the process visible as a flame on satellite imagery. Communities nearby can be exposed to harmful emissions.

💡 Use the Distance Analyzer to measure how far any flare is from a settlement.
